Bagian 1: Memahami Model Narrative Text

The Legend of Rara Jonggrang & Prambanan

Long ago in Java, there were two neighboring kingdoms: Pengging and Prambanan. The king of Pengging had a powerful prince named Bandung Bondowoso. Bandung defeated Prambanan in war and killed its king. When he saw the beautiful princess of Prambanan, Rara Jonggrang, he wished to marry her.

Rara Jonggrang did not want to marry the man who had defeated her father. However, she was afraid to refuse directly. Therefore, she set a impossible condition: "You must build one thousand temples in a single night before dawn." Bandung accepted the challenge and called upon a army of spirits and genies to assist him.

By midnight, the spirits had built 999 temples very quickly. Seeing this, Princess Rara Jonggrang became worried. She woke up the village maidens and asked them to pound rice and burn straw in the east to trick the roosters into crowing early. Thinking morning had arrived, the spirits panicked and fled, leaving the 1000th temple unfinished.

When Bandung Bondowoso discovered her trickery, he became furious. He cursed Rara Jonggrang, saying, "Since you tricked me, you shall become the final statue!" She transformed into a stone statue, completing the thousandth temple now known as Candi Prambanan.

Bagian 2: Menganalisis Cerita Naratif

Analisis Teks: The Story of Ashabul Kahfi (The Cave Sleepers)

Once upon a time in ancient Ephesus, a group of brave young men stood up for their faith against a tyrannical ruler named Decius, who forced everyone to worship idols. Refusing to abandon their beliefs, the youths escaped the city and sought shelter inside a dark cave in the mountains.

As they fell exhausted, God caused them to fall into a deep, miracle sleep that lasted for over 300 years, while their faithful dog guarded the cave entrance. When they finally woke up thinking they had slept only a day, one youth went to the marketplace with old silver coins to buy halal food, only to realize centuries had passed and their faith was now embraced by the entire land.
